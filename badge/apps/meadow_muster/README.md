# Meadow Muster

Meadow Muster is a timed herding game where you guide skittish critters into a fenced pen.

Press **Select** to start. Move the shepherd with the **D-pad**: critters run
away when you get close. Approach from the opposite side of the destination to
steer them through the bottom gate. The gaps on the left and right are escape
routes.

Get at least half the flock into the pen before the timer expires. A round
ends when time runs out or every critter has been penned or escaped. Each
capture earns points, and clearing a round early earns a time bonus. Press
**Select** after clearing a round to face a bigger or faster flock.

Use **Menu** to pause, **Back** from pause to return to the title, or **Home**
to exit. The badge saves your best score. No Wi-Fi is required.
